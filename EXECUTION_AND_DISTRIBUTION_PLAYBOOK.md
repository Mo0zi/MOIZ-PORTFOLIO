## 3. Article Distribution Workflow (Medium, Dev.to & Hashnode)

### Dev.to Syndication Protocol:
1. Log in to [Dev.to](https://dev.to/) and click **Create Post**.
2. Copy Markdown content directly from `articles/XX-filename.md`.
3. In frontmatter, set:
   ```yaml
   title: "Article Title"
   published: true
   tags: agenticai, langchain, python, webdev
   canonical_url: https://www.moizahmed.online/#blog-slug-name
   ```
4. Publish article and share link on LinkedIn & Twitter.
