Building successful digital products requires mastery across frontend interfaces, backend servers, database optimization, and deployment DevOps.

Depending on business goals, choosing the right stack is critical:

- **MERN Stack (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js)**: Ideal for real-time AI SaaS, interactive dashboards, and cloud applications.
- **PHP MVC + MySQL**: Ideal for enterprise B2B wholesale portals, ERP systems, and high-SKU inventory catalogs requiring sub-150ms TTFB.
