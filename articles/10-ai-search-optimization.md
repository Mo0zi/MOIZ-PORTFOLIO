Search engines are transforming from traditional list pages into conversational answer engines powered by Large Language Models (*ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude*). 

To capture organic visibility in 2026 and beyond, web applications must implement **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** and **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)**.

## 3 Core GEO Execution Strategies

1. **Factual Definition Lead Paragraphs**: Format section introductions in direct 40-word definition syntax that LLMs can extract verbatim.
2. **Schema.org Knowledge Graph Integration**: Link all entities (`Person`, `Organization`, `LocalBusiness`, `Service`) via unique `@id` anchors.
3. **Allow AI Bots in Robots.txt**: Ensure `robots.txt` explicitly allows GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, and Google-Extended crawlers.
