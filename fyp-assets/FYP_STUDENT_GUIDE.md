## 🎯 5 High-Scoring FYP Project Categories for 2026

### 1. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Document Search Systems
- **Recommended Stack**: Python, LangChain, FAISS Vector Search, Google Gemini API, React.js
- **Key Highlight**: Allows enterprise users to query private PDF documents with sub-300ms vector search and zero data leakage.

### 2. Autonomous Agentic AI Multi-Agent Workflows
- **Recommended Stack**: Python, OpenAI API, LangChain Agents, FastAPI, React.js
- **Key Highlight**: Multi-agent delegation chains for automated research, coding, or marketing content creation.

### 3. Industrial Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Systems
- **Recommended Stack**: PHP MVC, MySQL, JavaScript, HTML5/CSS3
- **Key Highlight**: Real-world piece-rate worker wage ledgers, lot completion tracking, and transaction-safe financial accounting.

### 4. Custom B2B & B2C eCommerce Platforms
- **Recommended Stack**: React.js / Next.js, Node.js, Express, MongoDB
- **Key Highlight**: Unified pricing tier models for wholesale B2B and retail B2C shoppers.

### 5. High-Security Institutional Management Portals
- **Recommended Stack**: PHP MVC, MySQL, Bitwise RBAC, CSRF Protection Subsystem
- **Key Highlight**: Bitwise bitmask authorization engines enabling O(1) permission checks.

## 💡 How to Prepare for Your FYP Viva Defense

1. **System Architecture ERD**: Master your database schema normalization and index keys.
2. **Algorithm Justification**: Be ready to explain *why* FAISS cosine similarity was chosen over Pinecone or SQL keyword search.
3. **Live Demonstration**: Host your project live on VPS infrastructure (DigitalOcean / VPS / Vercel) rather than relying solely on localhost.
